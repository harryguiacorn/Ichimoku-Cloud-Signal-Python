Filter guide

For numeric columns:

  • 1 matches exactly 1
  • >1 or 1+ matches values greater than 1
  • 0+ matches positive values; 0- matches negative values

Comparisons such as >=1, <1, and <=1 are also supported. Text searches match partial values, ignoring case.
